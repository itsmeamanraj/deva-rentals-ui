About Us

Abhaya

What we do

Abhaya is a family-owned company headquartered in Chennai, India, founded in 1959 by Shri Kawarlal Jain, a visionary philanthropist.

We are a leading supplier of high quality ingredients sourced globally serving Pharma, Food, Nutra and Cosmetics.

Mission and Vision

To create healthy and quality lifestyle with an extensive network across the globe in Pharma, Food and Nutra by sourcing ethically and delivering the right value at the right time.
